# Ganymede — ADR 000: Template

Use this template for every material technical decision. An ADR is written before implementation, not after.

## Context

What is being decided, and why now. What is the problem or opportunity. What is the scope of the decision. What happens if we defer it.

---

## Decision

What we are doing. Be specific. Include the choice, the mechanism, and the boundary conditions.

---

## Consequences

What this enables. What it costs. What becomes easier. What becomes harder. What is now explicitly out of scope.

---

## Alternatives considered

What else was considered and why it was not chosen. Include the alternative, the reason it was rejected, and any condition under which it would be reconsidered.

---

## References

Links, documents, or prior art that informed the decision.

---

## Notes

Any caveats, open questions, or follow-up work.

---

*ADRs are the record of how the architecture got to where it is. They are not chitchat. If a decision is not material enough to write down, it is not material enough to need an ADR.*
