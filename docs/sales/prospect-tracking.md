## 30-account prospect list

Build a named list of 30 Texas civil-litigation firms. For each, record:

- Firm name and geography.
- Practice type and approximate size (10-50 attorneys target).
- Named buyer role: managing partner, litigation partner, operations lead, or IT/MSP contact.
- Introduction path: warm intro, cold outreach, referral, community contact.
- Likely matter volume and document workflow fit.
- Whether a safe corpus is plausibly available.
- Whether there is a budget owner or procurement path.
- Last contact date and next step.

**File:** `docs/sales/prospect-list.md` (or a spreadsheet if preferred).

**Do not** publish the list or share it beyond the founder. It is a working document.

## Discovery interview script

Full script is in [Interview Script](../discovery/interview-script.md). The short version for outreach:

1. You're researching how civil-litigation teams handle document-heavy matters — chronologies, internal memos, fact-finding across PDFs and scans.
2. You're not selling anything today. You want to understand one recent matter they worked on.
3. You want to know where the time went, what they'd distrust immediately, what data can never leave their control, and whether a safe pilot corpus exists.
4. If it fits, you'll ask whether they'd review a prototype or a citation sample before committing.

## Outreach template (first contact)

Subject: private matter workspace — research, not a pitch

"I'm researching how Texas civil-litigation teams handle document-heavy matters — specifically chronologies, internal memos, and fact-finding across PDFs, scans, and Word files. I'm not selling anything right now. I'd like 30 minutes to walk through one recent matter you worked on and understand where the time went, what you'd distrust in an answer immediately, and what data can never leave your control. If it's a fit, we can talk about a bounded paid pilot later. If not, I'll note the disqualifier and move on.

Would a 30-minute call this week or next work?"

## Commitment advance definitions

| 10 | 3 | 1 |
|----|---|---|
| qualified contacts | live conversations | commitment advance |

A **qualified contact** is a partner, operations leader, or legal technologist in the target profile.

A **live conversation** is a workflow-first call with no generic product pitch.

A **commitment advance** is one of: corpus review, technical meeting, proposal, or signature.

---

## Buyer evidence ladder

| Weak | Useful | Strong |
|------|--------|--------|
| "Interesting," newsletter signup, social reaction, feature suggestion. | Introduces IT, offers a corpus, schedules reviewers, shares current cost. | Reviews terms, names budget, signs pilot, or pays. |

Use this to score each prospect weekly. Move them up only when the evidence changes.

---

## Qualification filter

A valid design partner has:
- A recurring document-heavy workflow.
- A named sponsor.
- A safe corpus (synthetic, public, or expressly authorized).
- An IT/security path.
- Authority to buy.

Any one missing is a disqualifier — not a maybe.

---

## Weekly prospecting target

10 qualified contacts · 3 live conversations · 1 commitment advance.

---

## What not to do

- Do not demo before mapping the workflow.
- Do not send a generic pitch deck.
- Do not pursue a prospect that needs licensed case-law content on day one.
- Do not pursue a prospect that expects autonomous legal advice or guaranteed accuracy.
- Do not pursue a prospect that cannot identify a budget owner or procurement path.
- Do not offer a free pilot to a firm that cannot buy.
