## Field legend

- **firm** — firm name.
- **geo** — Austin-area geography.
- **practice_note** — publicly visible practice focus, summarized from the firm's own materials or a directory profile.
- **attorney_count** — verified (single public source) or inferred (multiple public signals, not a confirmed count).
- **buyer_role** — likely sponsor / approver role to target.
- **intro_path** — how we would approach them.
- **workflow_fit** — why they may have the document-heavy workflow we're targeting.
- **safe_corpus_plausible** — whether a synthetic/public/authorized pilot corpus seems plausible.
- **budget_path** — whether a budget owner or procurement path seems present (inferred from firm size/structure; not confirmed).
- **last_contact** — blank until contacted.
- **next_step** — outreach draft ready (for top 8), or prospect-contact ready.

**Rule:** No attorney names, no invented attorney counts, no invented practice details. Where a count or detail could not be verified, it is marked inferred and sourced.

## How to read the counts

- **Verified:** Appears in a single public source (Super Lawyers profile, firm website, U.S. News, attorneys.org, Austin Bar Association directory). These are still directory figures, not internal headcounts. Treat as a signal, not a fact.
- **Inferred:** Multiple public signals point to a size band, but no single source confirmed a count. Do not treat as fact.
- **Below band:** Some firms on this list are likely below the 10-50 attorney target. They are included because they have a visible civil/commercial litigation practice and may still be worth a conversation; the disqualifier is not firm size alone, but whether they have the workflow, budget path, and authority to buy.

---

## Disqualifiers to check during outreach

For each prospect, before scheduling, confirm:
- Recurring document-heavy workflow.
- Named sponsor.
- Safe corpus (synthetic, public, or expressly authorized) is plausible.
- IT/security path exists.
- Authority to buy (budget owner or procurement path).

Any one missing is a disqualifier, not a maybe.
