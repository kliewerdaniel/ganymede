# Ganymede — Pilot Scorecard

**Status:** Draft
**Date:** September 2026
**Completed:** End of pilot (Week 12)

---

The pilot scorecard is completed at the end of the 30-day pilot. It is the evidence for the go/narrow/fix/stop decision.

---

## Pilot parameters

- **Pilot duration:** 30 days.
- **Matter:** one approved matter.
- **Users:** up to 10 named users.
- **Price:** $4,500 fixed, credited toward an annual agreement if converted.
- **Start date:** —.
- **End date:** —.
- **Sponsor:** —.
- **Technical contact:** —.

---

## Activation

- **Active users in first seven days:** target 3+.
- **Active users in two separate weeks:** target 3+.
- **Meaningful tasks completed:** target 20+.
- **Weekly use:** did users return without founder prompting?

---

## Quality

- **Citation support:** target 90% of material claims supported by reviewed sources.
- **Unsupported material claims:** target <5%.
- **Retrieval recall:** Recall@5 on the gold set (reference, not necessarily re-run if the corpus is frozen).
- **Matter isolation:** no cross-matter access failure.
- **Audit completeness:** 100% of key events reconciled.

---

## Reported value

- **Median reported time saved:** target 30% or more on the chosen workflow.
- **Outputs useful after edits:** target 70% or more.
- **User ratings:** collect per-task ratings and notes.

---

## Trust

- **Every exported artifact reconstructs its source and approval lineage.**
- **Citations are inspectable in context.**
- **Every factual answer links to the exact source passage.**
- **If evidence was absent, the system said so.**

---

## Reliability

- **No unresolved critical security defect.**
- **No unrecoverable data loss.**
- **Restore and backup behavior observed as expected.**

---

## Closeout

- **Value:** what was useful, what was not.
- **Risk:** what concerns remain.
- **Missing requirements:** what the buyer needed that was not delivered.
- **Budget:** can the buyer name a realistic annual budget?
- **Procurement path:** how would a purchase happen?
- **Willingness to continue:** written continuation decision or specific, categorized loss reason.

---

## Decision

| Outcome | Evidence | Next move |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| Go | Repeated use + quality pass + paid continuation | Onboard two more firms; harden the same workflow |
| Narrow | One artifact wins; broader workspace does not | Make that artifact the product |
| Fix | Demand exists; retrieval or deployment misses | Four-week reliability sprint; no new features |
| Stop | No recurring use or budget after valid trial | Interview loss reasons; choose a new vertical |

---

## Pilot scorecard threshold

The pilot is a success if:
- At least 3 active users in two separate weeks.
- At least 20 meaningful tasks completed.
- At least 70% of rated outputs marked useful after edits.
- Median reported time saved of at least 30% on the chosen workflow.
- At least 90% of material claims supported by reviewed sources.
- No cross-matter access failure and no unresolved critical security defect.
- A written continuation decision or a specific, categorized loss reason.
