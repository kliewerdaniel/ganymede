## Private Matter Intelligence — Design-Partner Pilot

A private matter workspace that helps your litigation team find facts, assemble chronologies, and draft from the record you already own — without sending client files to a public model.

---

## What you get

**One matter. One private deployment. Ten users. Thirty days.**

- A single-tenant workspace deployed in your controlled environment.
- Ingestion of your matter PDFs, scans, Word files, and text — with page-level provenance.
- Grounded Q&A: every answer links to the exact source passage. If the record doesn't support it, the system says so.
- Chronology and issue/evidence table built from your documents, editable by your team.
- Internal memo drafting from reviewed facts and citations only.
- Full audit trail: every upload, parse, retrieval, generation, edit, approval, and export is recorded.

---

## The three guided tasks

We pick three real tasks from your workflow and run them together:

1. **Find a fact** — locate a specific fact across the matter, with citation.
2. **Build the record** — produce a reviewable chronology or issue table.
3. **Start a draft** — produce an internal memo from the matter record.

---

## What we need from you

- A matter you are willing to use for the pilot, with documents you can share.
- A synthetic, public-domain, or expressly authorized corpus — not live client data without written terms.
- A named sponsor, up to ten users, and a technical contact who can approve the deployment.
- A pre-pilot baseline: how long the three tasks usually take, and what the current process is.

---

## What you get at the end

- A pilot scorecard: activation, weekly use, task completion, ratings, edits, reported time.
- A quality review: citation support, unsupported-claim rate, matter isolation, and audit completeness against our internal thresholds.
- A closeout conversation: value, risk, missing requirements, budget, procurement path, and whether to continue.

---

## Price

**$4,500 fixed, 30 days.** Credited toward an annual agreement if you convert.

The price covers onboarding, deployment support, three guided tasks, weekly review, and the final scorecard. It does not cover customer hardware, cloud infrastructure, unusual document migration, or bespoke integrations.

---

## Boundaries

- No general case-law research. No docket scraping. No e-discovery. No billing integration. No email sync. No mobile app. No shared multi-tenant SaaS.
- No model fine-tuning on your matter. No autonomous external action.
- No claim of legal accuracy or compliance. The product exposes evidence so your lawyers can evaluate their own professional duties.
