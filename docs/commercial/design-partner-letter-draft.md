Thank you for the conversation on **[date of interview]**. I appreciate the time you took to walk through a recent matter and the candor you showed about where the time went and what would make you distrust an answer immediately.

As we discussed, I am building a private matter workspace — a single-tenant, customer-controlled environment that helps litigation teams find facts, assemble chronologies, and draft internal memos from the record they already own, with every answer tied to the exact source passage.

What I am asking you to consider is a **design-partner pilot**: a 30-day, bounded, paid engagement on one matter, with up to 10 named users, onboarding, three guided tasks, weekly review, and a final scorecard. The pilot fee is $4,500, fixed, credited toward an annual agreement if you choose to convert.

This letter is not the pilot agreement. It is a request for your written commitment to participate in a pilot review, subject to the terms that counsel will finalize.

---

## What I am asking you to commit to

If you agree to proceed, I am asking you to:

1. **Identify one matter** you are willing to use for the pilot, with documents you can share for the pilot.
2. **Confirm the corpus is safe for the pilot** — synthetic, public-domain, or expressly authorized under written terms. No live client data will be used without written permission and handling terms.
3. **Name a sponsor** who owns the problem and can decide whether to proceed.
4. **Name up to 10 users** who will participate.
5. **Name a technical contact** who can approve the deployment.
6. **Agree to a pre-pilot baseline** — how long the three guided tasks usually take, and what the current process is.
7. **Participate in the three guided tasks** — find a fact, build the record, start a draft — with my support during onboarding.
8. **Participate in weekly review** during the 30-day pilot.
9. **Complete a final scorecard** at the end of the pilot: activation, weekly use, task completion, ratings, edits, reported time, citation support, unsupported-claim rate, matter isolation, and audit completeness.
10. **Give a written continuation decision or a specific, categorized loss reason** at the end of the pilot.

---

## What you will receive

- A single-tenant deployment in your controlled environment.
- Ingestion of your matter documents with page-level provenance.
- Grounded Q&A: every answer links to the exact source passage. If the record does not support an answer, the system says so.
- A reviewable chronology and issue/evidence table, editable by your team.
- Internal memo drafting from reviewed facts and citations only.
- A full audit trail: every upload, parse, retrieval, generation, edit, approval, and export is recorded.
- Onboarding, three guided tasks, weekly review, and a final scorecard.
- A quality review against our internal thresholds.

---

## What you will not receive

- General case-law research.
- Docket scraping, e-discovery, billing integration, email sync, or mobile app.
- Shared multi-tenant SaaS, model fine-tuning on your matter, or autonomous external action.
- Any claim that the product practices law, produces legally accurate conclusions, or automatically satisfies professional duties.
- A free pilot. This is a paid engagement. If you cannot buy, this is not the right fit.

---

## Boundaries I will respect

- I will not send your matter data to a public model provider.
- I will not demo before we have mapped a real workflow.
- I will not pursue this if your use case requires licensed case-law content on day one, or if you expect autonomous legal advice or guaranteed accuracy.
- I will not proceed without a safe corpus and without written terms if real matter documents are involved.
